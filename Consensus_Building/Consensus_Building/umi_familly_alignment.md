# UMI Family Alignment

**Tool:** MAFFT version 7.453

Parameters used:

### **--localpair**

Uses a sensitive local alignment strategy, improving alignment accuracy for sequence families that may contain localized regions of variation.

### **--maxiterate 500**

Performs iterative refinement for up to 500 cycles to optimize the multiple sequence alignment and improve overall alignment quality.

---

The alignment visualizations can be opened interactively in a new browser tab.

The aligned FASTA sequence files can be downloaded directly by clicking the FASTA links.
